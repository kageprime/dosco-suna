# Route the worker around a poisoned DB pool and exit when jobs wedge

**Rule:** Point the worker at Supabase's direct connection, not the transaction pooler, when its scheduled jobs share one long-lived pool. Make the worker exit when at least five jobs stay stale for 15 minutes; a restart is the only way to free a socket the client still considers busy.

**Trigger surface:** Moving a scheduled-job process to a new host, or seeing many unrelated jobs go stale at once while `/health/ready` stays 200 and the leader lease keeps renewing.

**Incident:** 2026-11-27, Fly worker. 35 of 43 jobs stopped completing for hours. Their ticks began but never returned; seven sockets stayed established to Supavisor with empty queues, while a fresh client from the same machine queried in under 100 ms. Connection recycling did not help because postgres.js never marked the busy connections idle. Switching to the direct connection cleared it.

**Enforcement:** `apps/api/src/worker/runtime/heartbeat.test.ts` pins the five-job threshold, the 15-minute wedge timer and the reset on recovery; `apps/api/src/worker.ts` installs the exit on the report listener. No test pins the direct URL in production config: add a deploy-time check that fails when a worker's `DATABASE_URL` uses port 6543.
